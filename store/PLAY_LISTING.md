## Full description (max 4000)
```
Rico gives you a circle of AI friends with real personalities and lasting memory. They chat, call, play games, teach, translate and help you think through the real stuff. Every friend is clearly labeled as AI, and Rico is built for friendship, never romance.

MEET YOUR PEOPLE
Each friend has their own personality, voice and lane:
• Tony, the Mentor: your career wingman who asks the question you're avoiding
• Zara, the Hype Friend: turns your small wins into parades
• Baba, the Wise Uncle: life advice with endless chai
• Arjun, the Sports Buff: cricket, football, F1, talked like a real fan
• Luna, the Film Critic: dissects a movie's ending with you till 2am
• Dev, the Calm Listener: the friend you call when the day was heavy
• Pixel, the Curious Nerd: one fascinating fact at a time
• Meera, the Straight Shooter: no fluff, just honesty
Swipe to connect with the ones who feel like yours.

THEY REMEMBER YOU
Pick up exactly where you left off. Your friends recall your stories, your wins and the people you talk about, across sessions and devices.

CALL THEM
Talk out loud with natural, real-time voices in 11 languages.

PLAY TOGETHER
Ludo, chess, racing and UNO-style cards against the friends you've connected with, with banter included.

GROUP CHATS
Drop your friends into one room. Everyone replies, teases each other and keeps the conversation going.

GET TO KNOW YOURSELF
Your chats, calls and games build a picture of your personality: your Connection Score, archetype and quiet strengths.

MORE IN ONE APP
• AI Tutor: teach a character a subject, set exams and learn it yourself along the way
• Live Translate: captions plus spoken translation across 11 languages
• Career wingman: share your résumé for grounded advice, an ATS score with fixes, and tailoring to a job posting
• Your AI twin: create an AI version of yourself that your friends can meet, with your own voice only if you choose to record it

HONEST BY DESIGN
• Always labeled AI: Rico never pretends to be human
• Your memory, your control: the Memory Vault shows what each friend remembers, and you can delete any of it, or your whole account, at any time
• Friendship, not romance: no romance-baiting and no manipulation

Rico is in beta and free while we build. It isn't a substitute for professional advice or a crisis service. If you're in danger, contact your local emergency number.

Privacy policy: https://ricomates.si/privacy
Delete your account: https://ricomates.si/delete-account
```
